ABOUT THE COMPANY

Building projects.
Building relationships.

Since first opened in 2006, Golden Pacific Trading and Contracting L.L.C. has earned a reputation for integrity, quality, service and attention to detail. Its growth from a local builder into one of Oman’s reputed construction companies reflects long-standing client relationships and a personalized approach to service.

Golden Pacific is committed to personalized, innovative, quality-driven solutions that reflect customer expectations and deliver strong outcomes. Every new project is an opportunity to build lasting relationships.
